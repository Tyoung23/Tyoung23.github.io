Detroit-Style Pizza

Detroit-style pizza is a work of art and I’ve been determined to get my recipe better and better every year! It’s thick, almost focaccia-like in height, but lighter and airier than it looks. The bottom crisps deeply, the edges caramelize with cheese, and the interior stays soft and structured instead of dense.
